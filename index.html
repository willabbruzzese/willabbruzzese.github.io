About

Junior physics student building autonomous UAV guidance systems. I spent nearly four years as a wilderness search-and-rescue team leader in Multnomah County, where drone support was routinely lagging behind search operations. Most of what I build now is aimed at that gap; guidance and natural-language control that let the person running the search operate the aircraft themselves. It's the same problem I want to work on at greater distance and larger scale through the design of spacecraft systems.

Research · 2026

Autonomous UAV GNC Research

Principal Investigator & Lead Engineer · April 2026–present

Platform & Operator Interface

  • $5,000 institutional grant; field-tested with Multnomah County Sheriff's Office SAR in an active pilot program.
  • Built the aircraft from the airframe up — Pixhawk 6C flight controller, M10 GPS, 915 MHz SiK telemetry, modified X500 V2 frame; handled power distribution, ESC/motor integration, and airframe assembly.
  • Built the operator-facing UI with live map, voice input, mission log, touch-based search-area definition, and real-time telemetry over a MAVLink pipeline to the flight controller.

Guidance & Autonomy

  • Geometric guidance algorithms (rotating calipers, scanline polygon clipping) for terrain-optimized trajectories — reduced search time 9%.
  • Bayesian probabilistic search + Koopman kinematic models updating target zones via Probability of Detection metrics.
  • Three-tier natural-language command parser; validated over 25 autonomous test flights at 96% command-execution success.

Research · 2025

Rocket Avionics & Control Systems

Analog PID Control

  • Fully analog PID controller built from discrete op-amps and passives. No microcontroller in the loop, every term realized in hardware.
  • Designed and simulated the circuit in LTSpice, tuning proportional and derivative stages against the plant response before committing to hardware.
  • Bench-validated 2D state-space stabilization on a self-balancing platform, then laying out a custom PCB.
  • Doing it in analog forces the control theory into physical components

Flight DAQ & State Estimation

  • Arduino-based flight data acquisition payload with onboard IMU and barometric altimeter, logging through boost, coast, and recovery.
  • Flew the payload on an Estes Bull Pup out of Colorado Springs (1,840 m field elevation), capturing motor burn, apogee, and ejection events in the raw telemetry.
  • Fused barometer and accelerometer channels through a Kalman filter to reject high-frequency structural noise and reconstruct altitude and velocity profiles — resolving apogee at 226 m, 7.2 s after ignition.
  • Post-flight analysis in MATLAB and Python across 20+ launch and test launch trajectories

Artemis II — Orion Spacecraft

These frames follow Orion during Artemis II's trans-Earth coast, roughly halfway home from the Moon. Since no catalog carries a spacecraft at lunar distance, pointing came from ephemeris data generated through NASA's Horizons System and fed into TheSkyX. Three minutes separate each frame.
